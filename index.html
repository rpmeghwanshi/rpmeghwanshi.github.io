Brief Bio

I am a final-year Ph.D. Scholar in the Department of Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Jodhpur , Rajasthan, India.

I am jointly supervised by Dr. Amit Bhardwaj and Dr. Himanshu Kumar . My doctoral thesis investigates "Study of the Factors Related to Force Perception for Telepresence and Teleaction Applications."

My research lies at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Human Perception & Psychophysics, and Haptic Teleoperation. I investigate how humans perceive physical forces during dynamic interactions and construct computational models, perceptual deadzone structures, and psychophysical experiments to optimize teleoperation, surgical robotics, and immersive virtual environments.

My work has been accepted and presented at premier venues including IEEE World Haptics Conference (WHC), ACM Symposium on Applied Perception (SAP), and published in ACM Transactions on Applied Perception (TAP) and Experimental Brain Research.

I am currently wrapping up my doctoral dissertation and actively seeking postdoctoral opportunities in psychophysics, haptic perception, multisensory interaction, and immersive human-centered computing.

Research Interests

Human-Computer Interaction

Designing human-centered interactive systems, haptic feedback devices, and intuitive multisensory interfaces.

Human Perception & Psychophysics

Characterizing sensory limits, sensory discrimination thresholds, Weber fractions, and psychometric functions.

Haptic Interaction

Kinesthetic force feedback, cutaneous vibration, tactile apparent motion, and telepresence/teleaction (TPTA) systems.

Interactive & Multimodal Systems

Cross-modal integration combining haptic-sound and visual-sound perception in virtual and simulated environments.

Perceptual Modeling

Mathematical modeling of perceived force intensity, multidimensional deadzone boundaries, and continuous force discrimination.

Human-Centered System Design

Architecting delay-aware teleoperation and medical simulation tools informed by human perceptual limitations.

User Studies

Rigorous empirical human-subject trials using 2-AFC, 3I-3AFC transformed adaptive methods, and parametric statistical analysis.

Human-AI Interaction

Integrating adaptive machine learning models (SVMs, optimization) to personalize and predict human perceptual responses.
